FAQ

What this pilot is—and is not.

Is this affiliated with 1688 or Alibaba?

No. This is an independent sourcing research tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by 1688 or Alibaba.

Does the margin estimate guarantee my profit?

No. It is an estimate based on the values you enter. Freight, duties, returns, advertising and other costs may change the result.

Will you place orders or handle payments?

No. The first version is a research and decision tool only. It does not place orders or hold funds.

Where is my data stored?

The calculator runs in your browser. This validation prototype sends nothing unless you choose to open and send the early-access email.
